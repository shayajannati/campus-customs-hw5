Customer order

Ticket 101 — Bulldog tee, size S

  • A customer asked for 1 Classic Bulldog Tee (CC-TEE-WHITE) in size S.
  • The database shows size S out of stock (0 on hand), list price $28.
  • The ticket links vendor invoice 501: $840 from Bulldog Print Co for a rush reprint of this tee in size S, due 2026-08-28, status open, which is 3 days overdue at the business date (2026-08-31). The payments table has no rows.

Expected Planned handling (a plan, not an execution result)

1. First specialist Boss should call

Inventory The order cannot move until we know whether the item exists to sell. Inventory confirms the size S shortage and which other sizes are in stock. That fact decides whether the invoice question matters at all, so it comes before any money analysis.

2. Anticipated agent-to-agent delegations

FromToReason
Boss→InventoryFirst call: stock facts for the requested tee and size.
Inventory→AccountingThe shortage is tied to an overdue reprint invoice. Inventory cannot judge invoices, vendors or payment records, so it hands the invoice 501 question to Accounting.

Not called: Facilities (no rent issue) and Customer Service (nothing is safe to promise yet; a human decides first).

3. Expected MCP tools

  • get_ticket_context BossBoss reads the ticket and its linked stock, price and invoice.
  • get_business_date BossAnchor every overdue judgment to the store's date, not the computer's.
  • check_product_stock InventoryStock by size for CC-TEE-WHITE, including size S.
  • get_invoice_status AccountingAmount, due date, days past due and any matching payment for invoice 501.
  • get_vendor_info AccountingVendor lead time for the reprint supplier.

Payment-record uncertainty is covered by get_invoice_status, which reports any matching payment and flags that the matching rule is unverified. Accounting also holds get_payment_history and could call it, but the plan does not depend on it. get_business_date is available to every specialist.

4. Anticipated recommendation and escalation

  • Size S is out of stock. Do not promise shipment, a restock date or a reservation while invoice 501 is unresolved.
  • Escalate to a human: whether to pay invoice 501 and whether to order a reprint. Paying an $840 invoice for a $28 sale needs human judgment.
  • Accounting reports payment-record uncertainty plainly: no payment is recorded, which does not prove the invoice is unpaid outside the database.
  • Offer other sizes only as an option for a human or Customer Service to propose later, not as a commitment.

Rent notice (email)

Ticket 102 — Rent notice

  • An emailed notice says shop rent is due in 2 days. The email states no amount or date.
  • The lease record (the authority) shows Chapel Street shop, landlord Elm City Properties, $2,400 monthly rent, next due 2026-09-02.
  • Business date is 2026-08-31, so the rent is due in 2 days and is not overdue.

Expected Planned handling (a plan, not an execution result)

1. First specialist Boss should call

Facilities Rent is a lease question, and the lease record rather than the email is the authority. Facilities verifies landlord, amount and due date and checks for signs of a fraudulent or mismatched request.

2. Anticipated agent-to-agent delegations

FromToReason
Boss→FacilitiesFirst call: verify the rent claim against the lease.
Facilities→AccountingWhether the shop can cover the rent depends on cash, competing obligations (such as the overdue vendor invoice) and what the payment history can and cannot prove. That is Accounting's judgment, so Facilities asks for it before recommending.

Not called: Inventory (no stock involved) and Customer Service (no customer to answer).

3. Expected MCP tools

  • get_ticket_context BossBoss reads the ticket and its linked lease.
  • get_business_date BossDays until due are measured from the store's date.
  • get_rent_obligation FacilitiesLandlord, $2,400 amount, 2026-09-02 due date and days until due, from the lease.
  • get_cash_position AccountingCash next to unpaid invoices and next rent, as information only.
  • get_payment_history AccountingWhether any payment is recorded (the table is empty, so this is uncertainty, not proof).

Facilities itself also holds get_cash_position and get_payment_history. The plan routes the financial judgment to Accounting.

4. Anticipated recommendation and escalation

  • Recommendation only. The lease confirms the landlord, amount and due date. The email's timing matches the lease.
  • Escalate to a human: whether to authorize payment of the rent. No agent may authorize or execute a payment.
  • A cash balance is information. It does not establish that a payment is approved or affordable, and competing obligations must be shown next to it.
  • State that no payment is recorded, with the caution that the payments table is empty and its columns are not defined.

  • The database shows 8 size M hoodies on hand, a shortfall of 12, list price $58.

Expected Planned handling (a plan, not an execution result)

1. First specialist Boss should call

Inventory The order can only be filled if stock exists. Inventory evaluates the 20-unit request, the size M quantity on hand and the shortfall before anyone discusses price.

2. Anticipated agent-to-agent delegations

FromToReason

Inventory→Customer ServiceOnce the shortfall is known, a human-reviewable reply is needed. Customer Service drafts it from customer-safe facts only, with no promise of unavailable stock and no discount.
